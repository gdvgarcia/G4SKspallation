# G4SKspallation

Here you are going to find the files related to simulations of the muon induced spallation at the Super-Kamiokande water Cherenkov detector with the software Geant4 version 10.06.p01.

The first simulation consists of downward muons with the SK energy spectrum targeting the SK water tank (with no photomultiplier introduced) with a layer of 200 cm of water outside the detector to be the equivalent to 74 cm of rock as stated in the SK article, where we have taken as reference the CMS barrel hadron calorimeter which is about 79 cm deep and which is a sampling calorimeter made of brass and plastic scintillator tiles, becaue we want some neutrons to exist still and for distances of interaction larger than this length, we would have about no neutron arriving in the detector).

The second simulation adds the photomultipliers to the tank.

The third simulation adds the angular distribution of muons to the tank, but not the photomultipliers.
