# G4SKspallation

Here you are going to find the files related to simulations of the muon induced spallation at the Super-Kamiokande water Cherenkov detector with the software Geant4 version 10.06.p01.

The first simulation consists of downward muons with the SK energy spectrum targeting the SK water tank(with no photomultiplier introduced, but with a layer of 100 cm of water to be the equivalent to 37 cm of rock as stated in the SK article and taken as reference the CMS barrel hadron calorimeter is about 79 cm deep and which is a sampling calorimeter made of brass and plastic scintillator tiles, becaue we want some neutrons to exist still and larger than this length we would have about no neutron coming from the interaction).
